About ToolForge

ToolForge is an all-in-one collection of free online tools built for developers, designers, students, marketers and everyday users. Every tool runs locally in your browser — your files and text are never uploaded to a server.

Founded by Muneeb Ullah, ToolForge started with one idea: the tools scattered across dozens of websites should live in one fast, beautifully organised place — free, with no sign-up walls.

  • 120+ working tools across 12 categories
  • Private by design: processing happens on your device
  • Free forever for everyday use

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. ToolForge is designed to be private by architecture, not just by promise.

  • No uploads: file and text tools process data locally in your browser. Your content is never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Local storage only: favourites, recent tools and your theme choice are saved on your own device and can be cleared any time in your browser settings.
  • Third-party lookups: a few tools (DNS, My IP, currency rates, SSL lookup, code runners, PlantUML) contact the public API you asked them to query; only the value you enter (for example a domain name) is sent to that service.
